Top Dogs community rules

Top Dogs is optional. If your dog joins, other Pawprints users can see the name and title you choose and your dog's park visits. These rules apply to everyone who uses Top Dogs, including people who only view the boards.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Names must not be offensive, sexual, hateful, harassing or threatening, and must not refer to drugs or violence. To report a dog, long-press it or tap the ellipsis on its row or record, then choose Report. You can also block a dog the same way. A blocked dog shows as "Blocked user" on the boards you see, and its owner is not told.

I review reports within 24 hours. A name that breaks these rules is hidden, and a household that breaks them is removed from Top Dogs, which hides all of its dogs. Names are also checked automatically when they are saved.
